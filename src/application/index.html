Stealth mode

Hide the app from the Dock, Cmd+Tab, menu bar, and Mission Control. The floating overlay lets clicks pass through to your meeting, without taking focus. Use keyboard shortcuts to show, hide, move, scroll, and adjust it.

Screen-share protection is best effort. On macOS, full-screen sharing in Meet, Slack, or Teams may still show the overlay. Share a specific app window and verify the viewer’s screen before relying on it.

Press Option+Shift+S to restore the app. Restarting also restores normal mode.
